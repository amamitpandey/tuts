## Time management notes

- Multitasking : parallel more works, one need attention other not, like driving with music
- Switch tasking : need focus on all works, not good, reduce productivity, sleep(due to mind use to switch focus), increase working time, increase stress.

- Clear your mind by writing your task, don't see them every time.
- Focus more on your MVA(most valuable activities), delegate those things which are LVA(less valuable activities), compare like if I'm gonna do how much I'll pay and if others are doing how much they could take.
- Don't tell yes to every task, instead if saying no, ask question, is it urgent, if yes is it MVA, if yes when we can do it, if you get timeline, got grace time, sometime it auto resolve, or you will get time to think, is it doable by asking these questions.

### Gathering points:
points those attract your mind like mail's inbox, message, from your phone, from your computer, GP should be less then 6. combine your all inbox max 1-2, message box 1-2, calendar 1-2
keep calls for urgency, messages and mail for normal task.
- Keep two kinds of inbox, physical and digital, note physical task make it digital

### Gathering points processing:  
- process the task by categories it put it in their place, organize well, everyone has home, keep a specific time to process all categories which are MVA
- ask three 'W' what to do, when to do, where to do, then process it if it's just 15 mins tasks do it now otherwise plan it by setup the calendar.
- if it's daily based task keep those on separate page, so your eye don't see and mind start to calculating, so keep your brain clean, collect them on weekly basis and process it.
- If task is taking more than 15mins and urgent setup a time in calendar to do it, otherwise to it now, don't work on fake urgency.
- To do list: prepare three kind of list urgent, normal, later
- Notes: Keep notes for long time in notepad, save it on OneNote for long time
- Temp notes: write on normal notepad
- Process Gathering points, twice in week, once in the week, depends on free time
