## Summary of Conflict Resolution Styles

**COMPETING (Win : Lose)**

* **Level:** High Assertiveness, Low Cooperativeness


* **Motto:** *"I'm right, you're wrong"*

* **Focus:** Pursuing one's own concerns at the expense of others, using power or position to win.


**COLLABORATING (Win : Win)**

* **Level:** High Assertiveness, High Cooperativeness


* **Motto:** *"Let's work together"*

* **Focus:** Working together to find an innovative solution that fully satisfies the goals and concerns of both parties.


**AVOIDING (Lose : Lose)**

* **Level:** Low Assertiveness, Low Cooperativeness


* **Motto:** *"After you, no after you"*

* **Focus:** Sidestepping, postponing, or withdrawing from the conflict altogether without addressing either party's needs.


**ACCOMMODATING (Lose : Win)**

* **Level:** Low Assertiveness, High Cooperativeness


* **Motto:** *"You're right, I'm wrong"*

* **Focus:** Neglecting personal concerns to satisfy the needs and expectations of the other party to preserve peace or relationships.


**COMPROMISING (Win - Win / Middle Ground)**

* **Level:** Moderate Assertiveness, Moderate Cooperativeness (Center)


* **Motto:** *"Let's find the middle ground"*

* **Focus:** Finding an expedient, mutually acceptable solution where both sides give up something to gain a resolution.
